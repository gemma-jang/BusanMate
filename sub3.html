What is a Mate?

A Mate is a travel companion who helps you experience Busan like a local. They’re not a tour guide, but someone to explore, talk, and share moments with. Whether it’s walking the city, finding hidden spots, or just grabbing coffee, a mate makes your trip feel more personal and connected.
